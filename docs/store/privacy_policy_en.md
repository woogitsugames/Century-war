# Century War Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 31, 2026  
**Applies to:** Century War for Android, base version 0.1.0 (without online services).

## 1. Publisher and contact

Century War is developed under the **WooGitsu Games** label. The publisher shown on Google Play will be the data controller if online services are introduced in the future.

## 2. Data handled by the current version

**The current version does not transmit personal data or gameplay data to the publisher.** It does not require an account or sign-in and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking tools, or an active connection to an external server.

The app stores the following data **on your device only**:
- the nickname you choose (the initial commander name is localized to the game's language);
- campaign progress, stars, medals, research upgrades, and local leaderboard records;
- gameplay statistics and settings, including language, graphics quality, sound, and vibration.

These records are used to save your progress and operate game features. **The game does not send them to the publisher or sell them to third parties.** We do not request your legal name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.

## 3. Leaderboards and online features

In the current version, all leaderboards are **local to your device**. Google Play Games Services and a custom leaderboard server are **not enabled**.

A later release may offer voluntary online leaderboards. If you choose to enable them, the game may transmit a nickname, a randomly assigned profile identifier (UUID), a score, a challenge identifier, and the submission time. The backend may also process IP addresses in technical connection logs. UUIDs and nicknames are **pseudonymous** data, not necessarily anonymous data. If you choose Google Play Games integration, Google may process player identifiers and profile information under its own privacy policy.

**Before enabling online services**, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration, specify data recipients and retention periods, and explain how to opt out. Online features will not be required for offline gameplay.

## 4. Ads and purchases

The first version **does not contain ads or in-app purchases**. Optional rewarded advertising and cosmetic purchases are ideas for future releases only. Before enabling them, we will disclose the relevant SDK providers and their data processing practices.

## 5. Android backup

Depending on your device and account settings, Android may include local game data in a system backup. This is a system-level feature managed by you and the operating system provider; it does not grant the game publisher access to that backup. See Android backup settings and Google's privacy policy for more information.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until you remove it. The in-game progress reset clears achievements and statistics but **keeps your selected nickname**. To remove your nickname and all other local app data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Android backups may remain subject to Google's separate settings and retention rules.

The current version does not create accounts or store player records on publisher-operated servers, so there are no server-held player records to delete.

## 7. Security and children's privacy

Gameplay records are held in the app's private Android storage. The current version has no public player-to-player communications, personalized advertising, or external chat. Its age rating will be assigned through the IARC questionnaire based on the actual game content.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page, including its effective date, before adding features that change how user data is collected, used, or shared.
