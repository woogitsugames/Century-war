# Century War — Google Play listing (EN)

## Title
Century War: Era Strategy

## Short description
9-era strategy: commander, tower tiers and offline battles.

## Full description

Lead an army from tribal warriors to the orbital age. In Century War, two armies fight for a single front line. Train troops, build turrets, launch barrages and decide when to advance to the next era.

Every battle is about choices. Spend gold on more troops or strengthen your defenses? Upgrade your current units or earn enough experience to advance? After the battle, invest your medals in permanent research.

CHOOSE YOUR STRATEGY
- 9 eras and 36 units — from tribes through bronze, iron and steam to the orbital age.
- 36 campaign stages — face changing rules, stage modifiers and stars to collect.
- Era variants — choose distinct specializations when advancing through selected eras.
- Dynamic weather — rain, wind and night affect the battlefield.
- Endless Siege — survive waves, face bosses and choose cards to strengthen your army.
- Daily Challenge and Weekly Siege — different setups, results and goals.
- Custom Skirmish — set the era range, opponent strength and personality, and battle modifiers.
- Tactical battles — turrets, special abilities, doctrines, targeted barrages and troop upgrades.
- Commander — summon a leader whose aura strengthens nearby troops.
- Tower tiers — upgrade purchased towers through three defensive tiers.
- Recorded soundtrack — a calm and a battle theme for every era, blending smoothly as the fight heats up.
- 13 research paths — use earned medals to improve your army between battles.
- Replays — save completed battles and watch them with playback speed and timeline controls.
- Campaign difficulty levels, unlockable banners and a post-battle chart.
- Export or import your profile; adjust colour options and use a left-handed layout.
- Tutorial, codex, 79 achievements, daily and weekly missions, and local records.
- Accessibility — colour-blind-friendly palettes and high contrast, larger text, fewer flashes and less screen shake, screen reader support in menus and settings.
- 10 supported languages, including English and Polish.

PLAY OFFLINE

Campaign, skirmishes, Endless Siege, challenges and local progress saving work without a constant internet connection. You can also resume an interrupted battle.

Enjoy distinctive flat-shaded, outlined artwork, adjustable graphics quality and a layout that adapts to phones and larger screens.

No ads, in-app purchases or multiplayer.

Build your army. Advance through the eras. Hold the line.
