# Century War — Google Play listing (EN)

## Title
Century War: Era Strategy

## Short description
9 eras, 36 units, 36 campaign stages. Strategy without internet.

## Full description

Lead an army from tribal warriors to the orbital age. In Century War, you fight for a single front line: train troops, build turrets, gain experience, and advance through the eras.

Gold strengthens your troops and defenses, experience unlocks new eras, and doctrines change your tactics. After each battle, earn medals and invest them in permanent research.

WHAT AWAITS YOUR ARMY?
- **9 eras** — from tribes through the bronze and iron ages to the orbital age.
- **36 units** — four roles per era: melee, ranged, heavy, and elite.
- **36 campaign stages** — battle modifiers and up to 108 stars to earn.
- **Endless Siege** — successive enemy waves, bosses, and a choice of army-enhancing cards.
- **Daily Challenge** — a shared battle setup and one ranked attempt per day; practice runs remain available.
- **Skirmish** — four difficulty levels.
- **Research** — 13 permanent upgrade paths.
- **Turrets, special attacks, and front-line control** — tactical choices in every battle.
- **Local leaderboards** — your own records in campaign, skirmish, endless siege, and daily challenges.
- **Local achievements and missions** — earn rewards and track your progress.
- **Codex and guided tutorial** — learn about units, their stats, and how to play.
- **Resume battles** — return to an interrupted fight.
- **10 languages** — English, Polish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Ukrainian, Dutch, and Turkish.

PLAY WITHOUT A CONSTANT CONNECTION

Campaign, Skirmish, Endless Siege, Daily Challenge, and local progress saving **work without internet access**. Short battles are well suited to playing while traveling. Adaptive graphics settings support less powerful phones, while the interface scales to larger screens and foldable devices.

In builds with **properly configured Google Play Games**, you can also use online leaderboards and synchronize your game profile in the cloud after Play Games authentication. Connecting to Google is not required for core gameplay. Achievements currently work **locally**; submitting unlocks to the Google Play Games achievements service is not yet supported.

After your first win, you can receive a **local Daily Challenge reminder**. Adjust the hour or turn reminders off in game Settings; newer Android versions require notification permission. Reminders do not use a push server.

The game has no ads, in-app purchases, or multiplayer.

Build your army. Advance through the eras. Hold the line.
