# Century War — Google Play listing (EN)

## Title
Century War: Era Strategy

## Short description
Battle through 8 eras with 32 units and a 32-stage campaign. Play offline.

## Full description
Lead an army from the first tribal warriors to the orbital age. Century War is a one-lane strategy game where every battle is a fight for control of the front line. Train troops, build defenses, earn experience, and advance into a new era.

Victory takes planning. Spend gold on units and defenses, unlock new eras with experience, and choose doctrines that change your tactics. After each battle, earn medals and invest in permanent research upgrades.

YOUR CAMPAIGN THROUGH HISTORY
• 8 distinct eras — from tribes and bronze to steel, silicon, and the orbital age.
• 32 units — four roles per era: melee, ranged, heavy, and elite.
• 32-stage campaign — battle modifiers, escalating challenges, and up to 96 stars to earn.
• Endless Siege — survive wave after wave, pick new doctrines, and beat your personal record.
• Daily Challenge — the same battle setup for every commander, with one ranked attempt per day.
• Skirmish — standalone battles across four difficulty levels.
• Research — 13 upgrade paths to develop your army between battles.
• Turrets, special abilities, and front-line control — make every tactical choice count.
• Local leaderboards — track your best results for the campaign, skirmishes, endless siege, and daily challenges.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Century War works offline. Play the campaign, skirmishes, Endless Siege, and Daily Challenge without an internet connection. Your progress and leaderboard records are stored on your device. The first release has no ads.

Short tactical battles fit into a busy day. Adaptive graphics settings help the game run on less powerful phones, while the interface scales for tablets and foldable devices.

Build your army. Advance through the ages. Hold the line.
