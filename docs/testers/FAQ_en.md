# Century War — tester FAQ

This guide is for the test build. The person running the test will provide the joining link and feedback channel; they are not included here.

## 1. How do I join and install the game?
Open the Google Play test link you received, join as instructed on the page, and install from the store. Use the account named in your invitation. If you cannot see the game, check the account and tell the test organizer.

## 2. What should I test?
Play normally and note anything unclear, too difficult, inconvenient, or broken. Feedback about the early stages, battle pacing, and performance on your phone is especially useful.

## 3. Where is the Tester report?
In the game, open **Settings → About → Tester report**. You may need to scroll. A description of the data appears before you choose whether to share it.

## 4. How do I send the report?
In the report dialog, tap **Share** and choose the app and recipient given by the organizer. You can also copy the text from the system share sheet and paste it into a form if the organizer provides one. **Copy and open form** appears only if the test build has a form address configured.

## 5. When should I send it?
Please send a report on days 3 and 10 of the test and when a serious problem occurs. A reminder may appear in the test build’s menu; you can open the report yourself without waiting for it.

## 6. Where is my tester code?
The test-build report contains a four-character **Tester code** field. Enter it in the form if the organizer asks. The report is shared only when you choose to send it.

## 7. Why is there a code, and will it stay the same?
The random code helps match reports from different days. It is stored in the local test log and may change if you clear app data or reinstall. If that happens, let the organizer know through the agreed feedback channel.

## 8. What does the report contain?
It includes the game and Android versions, device model, progress, play time, modes, performance, and settings, including whether TalkBack is on. The test build also includes the code, crash and nonresponse counts, a map of days played, research levels, and survey answers. You can review the text before sending it.

## 9. What is not in the Tester report?
It does not include your in-game nickname, player ID, or Play Games account. The tester code is a separate random identifier, so we do not call the report completely anonymous. The game does not send it automatically; services and apps you choose have their own policies.

## 10. What should I do after a crash?
Start the game again. If **A crash was detected** appears, you can tap **Share** and send the report to the organizer. If it does not appear, use **Settings → About → Report a problem**. Describe what you were doing before the crash and whether you can reproduce it.

## 11. What is in a crash report?
The technical report may include the game version, device model, Android version, screen, recent technical events, and an error stack or process-exit reason. It excludes your nickname and player ID. It is stored locally, and you decide whether to share it.

## 12. How do I report a bug with a screenshot?
Take a screenshot using your phone’s normal controls. In your message or form, include the version shown under **Settings → About**, phone model, steps to reproduce, what happened, and what you expected. Attach the screenshot separately in your chosen app or form; check it for private information first.

## 13. Do I have to answer surveys?
No. In a test build, a short survey and later a final survey may appear in the menu. Answers stay in the local log and may reach the organizer when you choose to share the report.

## 14. How do I back up my profile?
In **Settings**, expand **Advanced**, find **Save profile to file** and choose **Export**. Save the file somewhere you choose. This is a profile backup; do not assume it includes an interrupted battle, local replays, or the test log.

## 15. How do I restore a profile?
In **Settings**, expand **Advanced**, select **Load profile from file → Import** and choose your saved file. Import replaces local progress; the game first attempts to save a copy of the current profile. **Undo last import** restores the previous valid backup if one is available.

## 16. How do I turn off reminders?
In **Settings**, switch **Notifications** to **Off**; this controls Daily Challenge notifications. Tester-report banners are separate reminders in the test build’s menu, not that setting. You can also manage app notifications in Android.

## 17. Do I need to be online to play?
Basic play and local progress work without a constant connection. Store installation and updates, and sending a report through your chosen service, require a connection; online features depend on the build’s configuration.

## 18. Where can I read the privacy policy and delete local data?
Open **Settings → About → Privacy policy**; if the policy URL is not configured, the game shows a local summary. You can clear local data in Android settings or uninstall the game. Android backups and Google service data may need separate handling; export your profile first if you want to keep your progress.
